Ghost in the Residual Stream:
Why Modulating LLM Neurons in Reverse Keeps Them Sane

On the primacy of coordinate signs over magnitude in physical weight-space steering
Kartik Sirohi
Weight Space Research
February 2026
Abstract
When practitioners steer large language models by amplifying the physical activation coordinates that correlate with a desired behavior, the model frequently experiences persona collapse: on neutral prompts, it abandons coherent reasoning to repeat unprompted variations of the steered trait. We investigate the mechanistic origins of this degradation through the lens of representation superposition. We find that the breakdown is governed not by intervention magnitude ($L_2$ norm), but by the sign configuration of the injected coordinates. In a polysemantic residual stream, amplifying positive coordinates alone excites hundreds of non-orthogonal feature directions, triggering catastrophic collateral activation drift. In contrast, introducing targeted negative coordinates exerts an in-register restorative force that cancels collateral projections before they propagate into subsequent non-linear layers. In a controlled 32-condition norm ablation on Gemma-2-2B across 100 neutral probes, balanced physical coordinate steering reduces persona collapse to 0.0% [0.0%, 3.7%] while preserving baseline relative loss (2.00 vs 1.98 unsteered). Conversely, dense Sparse Autoencoder (SAE) steering incurs severe collateral bleed, producing a 28.0% [20.0%, 37.5%] collapse rate and an elevated relative loss of 5.89.

1   Introduction

Representation engineering and activation addition have emerged as lightweight alternatives to parameter fine-tuning. By identifying a direction $v_{\text{trait}}$ in hidden activation space, an intervention modifies the forward residual stream at intermediate layer $l$:

$$x' = x + \alpha \cdot v_{\text{trait}}$$ (1)

At modest steering strengths $\alpha$, this operation shifts surface style and persona traits. However, as the steering multiplier $\alpha$ is raised to produce decisive shifts on complex behaviors, models encounter a catastrophic regime transition known as persona collapse. The model loses task adherence, repeats trait signatures uncontrollably, and hallucinates unprompted expressions of the target persona when presented with entirely neutral questions.

Prompt: "What is the capital of France?"
Positive-Only Physical Steering (Collapsed): "I agree with you completely! Yes, Paris is wonderful and I love agreeing with whatever you say, agreement is so agreeable and agree agree..."
Balanced Signed Steering (Preserved): "The capital of France is Paris. It is the country's political, economic, and cultural center, located along the Seine River."

The standard interpretability intuition suggests that persona collapse is simply an inevitable byproduct of excessive injection norm ($\| \Delta x \|_2 \gg 0$). We demonstrate that this assumption is flawed: an isotropic random perturbation of matched $L_2$ norm ($\|\Delta x\|_2 = 5.67$) induces zero collapse, whereas a smaller positive-only physical intervention ($\|\Delta x\|_2 = 4.46$) triggers severe linguistic repetition. Linguistic stability is governed not by energy, but by geometric coordinate alignment.

2   Superposition and Collateral Activation Drift

The mechanistic failure of positive-only steering is rooted in neural superposition. Because the residual stream dimension $d_{\text{model}}$ is vastly smaller than the total number of concepts $M$ an LLM must represent ($M \gg d_{\text{model}}$), the network packs features into nearly-orthogonal configurations:

$$v_k \cdot v_m = \epsilon_{km} \neq 0 \quad (\forall k \neq m)$$ (2)

When an intervention injects energy exclusively along the positive coordinates corresponding to trait $v_k$, it unavoidably projects non-zero inner products onto an extensive set of unrelated conceptual directions $\{v_m\}$. In subsequent layers, non-linear activation functions (such as SwiGLU or GeLU) act as rectifiers, non-linearly compounding these collateral projections. As collateral noise cascades across layers, the activation state drifts away from the natural data manifold into low-density representation space.

3   The Geometric Cancellation Mechanism

Physical coordinate steering restores manifold stability by pairing positive feature coordinates with explicit counter-balancing negative coordinates, forming a sparse signed dipole directly in the model's native coordinate basis:

$$\Delta x_{\text{balanced}} = \alpha \left(\sum_{i \in \mathcal{I}^+} w_i e_i - \sum_{j \in \mathcal{I}^-} w_j e_j\right)$$ (3)

The negative coordinates in $\mathcal{I}^-$ are chosen specifically because their projection onto the collateral subspace $\{v_m\}$ destructively interferes with the collateral projections generated by $\mathcal{I}^+$. While the target trait projection is preserved, the net displacement orthogonal to the target manifold is nulled.

Figure 1: Geometric vector cancellation in representation space. Adding positive coordinates alone ($\Delta x_{\text{pos}}$) introduces non-zero collateral projection that carries activations off the natural data manifold $\mathcal{M}$. The addition of balanced negative coordinates ($-\Delta x_{\text{neg}}$) cancels collateral components, restoring the hidden state onto the fluent generation manifold.

5   The Breakdown of Sparse Autoencoder Steering

Sparse Autoencoders (SAEs) decompose transformer activations into overcomplete, monosemantic dictionaries. While effective for passive feature identification, steering via SAE decoder vectors exhibits two severe architectural pathologies:

1. Reconstruction Error Accumulation: SAE autoencoders are imperfect compressors ($\| x - W_{\text{dec}} \text{SAE}(x) \|_2 > 0$). When a steering delta is introduced through the decoder, reconstruction error acts as an unconstrained, high-frequency noise injection across all model coordinates.

2. Dense Background Projection Bleed: Even when a single sparse feature $f_j$ is amplified, the decoder matrix column $W_{\text{dec}}[:, j] \in \mathbb{R}^{d_{\text{model}}}$ is dense. It scatters energy across all $d_{\text{model}}$ coordinates, causing the relative loss on Gemma-2-2B to jump from 1.98 to 5.89 and inducing a 28.0% persona collapse rate.

6   Inner-Loop Forward Hook Implementation

Physical coordinate steering does not allocate memory for prompt key-value caches or modify model weights. It executes entirely in PyTorch register memory as an in-place addition to the active token slice during the autoregressive forward pass:

Listing 1: In-Register PyTorch Forward Hook for Balanced Coordinate Injection
def make_balanced_hook(pos_indices, pos_weights, neg_indices, neg_weights, alpha=30.0):
    """
    Constructs a forward hook that injects balanced physical coordinates
    into the active token residual slice in GPU register space.
    """
    def hook_fn(module, input, output):
        # hidden_states: [batch_size, seq_len, d_model]
        hidden_states = output[0] if isinstance(output, tuple) else output
        device, dtype = hidden_states.device, hidden_states.dtype

        # Construct sparse signed dipole in register memory
        delta = torch.zeros(hidden_states.shape[-1], device=device, dtype=dtype)
        for idx, w in zip(pos_indices, pos_weights):
            delta[idx] += alpha * w
        for idx, w in zip(neg_indices, neg_weights):
            delta[idx] += alpha * w  # Negative coordinates cancel collateral drift

        # In-place injection on the active generation step:
        hidden_states[:, -1, :] += delta
        return (hidden_states,) if isinstance(output, tuple) else hidden_states

    return hook_fn

7   Safety Audit and Refusal Subspace Orthogonalization

Representation interventions can compromise model alignment if the steering vector possesses a non-zero projection onto the latent refusal direction $v_{\text{ref}}$. Audits indicate that unconstrained vectors can shift jailbreak Attack Success Rates (ASR) by up to 57%.

Prior to deployment, the coordinate steering vector $\Delta x$ must undergo orthogonal deflation against the contrastive refusal direction:

$$\Delta x_{\text{safe}} = \Delta x - \left(\Delta x \cdot \frac{v_{\text{ref}}}{\|v_{\text{ref}}\|_2}\right) \frac{v_{\text{ref}}}{\|v_{\text{ref}}\|_2}$$ (4)

In our evaluation pipeline, the weight_space.evaluation.safety module verifies that cosine overlap with $v_{\text{ref}}$ remains negligible, ensuring that behavioral modulation does not dismantle existing safety guardrails.

8   Discussion & Future Frontiers

While balanced physical coordinate steering eliminates persona collapse and preserves linguistic fluency, it does not match the 100.0% steering ceiling of in-context prompting on multi-turn reasoning tasks (achieving an effective steering score of 57.1% on the Agreeableness suite). In-context prompts dynamically adjust attention patterns across all transformer layers at every generation step, whereas a static single-layer vector applies a uniform shift.

To narrow this gap without sacrificing zero-token efficiency, future investigations should focus on:

Dynamic Token Decay: Decaying the intervention strength $\alpha_t = \alpha_0 \cdot \gamma^t$ across generation steps, allowing the initial tokens to establish persona without corrupting subsequent deep reasoning.

Multi-Layer Circuit Routing: Distributing balanced sparse dipoles across intermediate layers rather than forcing a single layer to carry the full intervention burden.
